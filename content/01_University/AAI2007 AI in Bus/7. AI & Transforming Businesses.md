#### Blue Ocean Strategy
Pursuit of differentiation and low cost to open up a new market space and create new demand.

It also about creating and capturing uncontested market space, thereby making the competition irrelevant.
	-[Blue Ocean Strategy](https://www.blueoceanstrategy.com/what-is-blue-ocean-strategy/)


#### Porter 5 Forces
Used to identify and analyze the forces that shape the competitive nature and intensity of a market or industry.

- Competition
- Threats of new entrants
- Supplier Bargaining power
- Customer Bargaining power
- Ability of customer to find product substitutes

> Businesses can use the model to determine the intensity of competition and potential profitability, helping them better understand where power lies in their sector.
> - [Porter's 5 Forces](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/p/porter.asp)


#### Flywheel
A model where small, compounding efforts create continuous momentum that drives growth.

Shifts focus from one-way "dead-end" to self-sustaining cycle


## Duel Transformation Framework
Designed to help companies survive industry disruption.

Requires leaders to simultaneously optimize their **existing** core business for **resilience** (Transformation A) and build an entirely **new** growth engine for the **future** (Transformation B), while leveraging the firm's **unique**, shared capabilities to **gain** a competitive edge.


## Pi-Shaped Career Strategy
Professional skill-building that focuses on developing 2 distinct areas of **deep** expertise (Vertical Leg) bridged by a broad, interdisciplinary understanding of other domains (Horizontal Bar)


### Horizontal Bar
Represents general knowledge and foundational skills across various disciplines.
Allows individuals to communicate effectively with different departments, collaborate seamlessly, and understand the bigger picture.

### Vertical Legs
Represents genuine, advanced mastery in 2 separate fields. This allows the professional to independently solve highly complex problems and bridge the gap between 2 previously disconnected areas.
