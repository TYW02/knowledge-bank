# 5 types of business structure in SG
- Sole-Proprietorship (1 Owner)
- Partnership (2 or more Owner)
- Limited Partnership (LP)
- Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)
- Company

Ownership: How many people have shares in the business ?
Legal Status: Is it a separate legal entity from its owner(s) ?
Tax: How are the profit taxed ?
Continuity in Law: When does the business cease to exist.

## Incorporation
Process of constituting a company as a legal corporation

Incorporated company becomes a separate legal entity which means the owners' personal assets are protected from the company's liabilities
> Any loss will not exceed the amount invested in the business, keeping personal assets of owners and investors off-limits

Company may own asset, incur debts and enter into contract on its own

ACRA holds repository of basic information of registered businesses and companies. Profiles are accessible for a fee and intended to uphold transparency and trust.

## Sole Proprietorship
Unincorporate business

**Does not** exist as a separate legal identity from owner, has **UNLIMITED liability**

Owner is personally liable for ALL debts and losses
Business can sue or be sued in owner's name

Profits from business are taxed at owner's personal income tax rates
Business exists so long the owner is alive and has desire to continue.


## Partnership
Unincorporate business
**Does not** exist as a separate legal identity, have **UNLIMITED liability**

Can have between 2 - 20 partners
Partners are personally liable for all debts and losses incurred
Partnership can sue or be sued in partners' name
Profits from partnership are taxed at partners' personal income tax rates

Business existence is subject to partnership agreement.

## Limited Partnership
Unincorporated Business
**Does not** exist as separate legal identity
MUST be minimum of 2 partners, with at least 1 general partner and 1 limited partner
General partner has UNLIMITED liability and can take part in management of LP
General partner can be individual or company

Limited partner has LIMITED liability and cannot take part in management of LP
Limited partner can be individual, company, or unregistered foreign company
LP can sue of be sued in firm's name
Profits from LP are taxed at partners' personal income tax rate (if individual) or corporate tax rate (in corporation)
Business existence is subject to partnership agreement.

## Limited Liability Partnership
Exist as a separate legal identity from partners hence, partners have LIMITED liability
Must be a minimum of 2 partners
Partners can be individual, local company, foreign company or another LLP
LLP can sue or be sued in firm's name

Profits from LLP are taxed at partners' personal income tax rate (if individual) or corporate tax rate(if corporation)
Business has perpetual succession until wound up or struck off

## Incorporated Company: Company Officers
Must appoint at least 1 director and company secretary

Director is responsible for charting company's strategic direction as well as to ensure the company complies legal requirements like the accuracy of financial statements and timely submission of annual filing.

Director has fiduciary duty to act in interest of company, honestly and in good faith

Company secretary is responsible for administration of company as well as to ensure all directors and shareholders are informed of their legal obligation.

## Incorporated Company: Share and Shareholders
Share is a portion of a company owned by a shareholder in exchange for financial contribution towards the company's share capital 

A shareholder has partial ownership in a company. Shareholder can be person, business or company

Ordinary shares carries **voting rights** and shall receive dividends.
Share of **Private Limited Company** (Pte Ltd) are not publicly traded and there can be a maximum of 50 shareholders
Most incorporated companies in Singapore are Pte Ltd
Share of Public Limited Company(Ltd) are publicly traded in a stock exchange and there can be more than 50 shareholders.

# What is in Industry
Companies operating in the same industry tend to face similar opportunities and challenges, hence they can evaluate the relative attractiveness of another company within that industry

A sector is a group of companies engaged in similar business activities. A sector is broader than industry. There can be many industries in 1 sector

Singapore Standard Industrial Classification (SSIC) is used by the government to classify business activities in the country.

IMDA is responsible for the development of ICT and Media Industry in Singapore
Envisage better quality lives for Singaporean enabled by technology
IMA actively promoting innovation and nurturing talents in emerging technologies


# What are Business Operating Activities ?
Operating Activities contribute to most of a business **cash flow and expenses**

Cash is lifeblood of business and there must be enough cash flow to pay expenses, repay investors and grow the business

Examples of Operating activities:
- Production of products and services
- Marketing
- Sales

Non-operating activities are investing and financing activities, unless the business is providing these as a service.


# Financial Accounting
Shows financial health of company over a period to external audience, shows past performance
Limited Liability must keep accounting records in exchange of that privilege
Public companies are required by law to publish audited financial statement in annual report

Financial statement contains, balance sheet, profit and loss statement, cash flow statement

Balance sheet show snapshot of company's assets, liabilities, and shareholder equity

Profit loss statement show revenue and cost from operating and non-operating activities over a specific period.

Cash flow statement shows movement of cash in and out of a company, immediate accessible capital is has from operating, investing and financial activities

# Management Accounting
Show financial information of company to help internal audience make better business decision for future.

Management accounting statement are **Budget statement** and **cashflow forecast**

Budget statement resembles Profit loss statement, show revenue and cost except that it is a forecast of future performance

Cash flow forecast projects cash requirements or working capital, over rolling 12 month basis, by forecasting movement of cash in and out of company from its operating activities.

# Operating Revenue
Income generated from operating activities

A company generates revenue by acting as a **retailer**, **wholesaler**, and or **distributor**

Retailer typically sells **small quantities** of product or services
Wholesaler typically sells products or service in **bulk**
Distributor typically represent manufacturers and are bounded by **exclusive agreement** to cover certain territory or segment of market, to sell product through retailers, and or wholesalers

Interest, dividend earned and profit from investment are not considered operating revenue as they are not part of a company's core business activities.


# Operating Expenses
Cost incurred from operating activities

Company needs to pay for materials, inventories, rental, utilities, manpower, equipment, transportation, marketing, and insurance to **produce or acquire** products and services to sell

Operating expenses can be **fixed** or **variable**

Interest charges, cost of borrowing and losses from disposal of asset are not considered operating expense as they are not part of company's core business activities

**Capital Expenses** are usually made to acquire or upgrade assets for long-term or investment purposes. (Buy van for delivery purpose)

# What is a value chain ?

Aims to give company a **competitive advantage** in the industry
> Full range of activities needed to create a product or service. 
> Maximise value for consumers for the least possible cost

Value chain splits company's activities into **primary activities** and **support activities**

Primary activities
- Inbound logistics
- Operations
- Outbound logistics
- Marketing and sales
- Service

Support activities
- Procurement
- Technical development
- Human resources management
- Infrastructure


# What is a supply chain ?
Aims to optimise the movement of raw materials and finished products and a more efficient production cycle.
> Network of companies and people involved in production and delivery of product or service

Involves network of suppliers, warehouses, transport companies, distribution centers and retailers

Supply chain starts when company **receives an order** to the **fulfillment** of that order to customer and involves coordination of **how and when products are produced** and **transported**

May involve product development, operations, distribution, finance and customer service.


# What is Competitive Advantage ?
Helps company to produce products and services **better or cheaper** and can be broken down into **comparative and differential** advantages
> Competitive advantage stems from many discrete activities.

Comparative advantage is a company's ability to **produce more effectively**, resulting in **higher profit margin**

Differential advantage is when a company's product is seen as **unique and higher quality**

Value chain focus less on cost reduction and more on **innovation and value** to customer
Value chain aims to give a company a **competitive advantage**

All value chain involves the supply chain but not all supply chain are value chain
