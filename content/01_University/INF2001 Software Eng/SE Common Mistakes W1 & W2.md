# Open-Ended

> [!question] A new coding method is 10% faster than the on currently used. Why might a software engineer still not adopt it? Give at least 2 considerations.
> > [!Answer]-
> > - **Cost of Training** staff on the **new method**
> > - Impact of **introducing new technology**, which causes **disruption**
> > - **Effect on maintenance**. Maintenance is about 67% of total cost, while coding is only about 5-8%. So a 10% faster coding method yields at most about a 2% reduction in total cost, and if it makes code harder to maintain, it could cost more overall.


## Observation

> [!question] An analyst follows a user through their entire workday to see the full workflow. This is called:
> - [ ] A. Passive Observation
> - [ ] B. Participant Observation
> - [ ] C. Shadowing
> - [ ] D. Focus Group
> > [!Answer]-
> > - C.
> > - **Passive Observation**: Analyst **watches silently without interfering**, but **not necessarily for a whole day**.
> > - **Shadowing**: **Following** a user **throughout their workday** to see the **full workflow**.

## Type of Prototype
> [!question] Which type of prototype is quickly built to explore ideas and then discarded ?
> > [!Answer]-
> > - **Throwaway/Rapid**: **Built quickly** to explore ideas, **then discarded**
> > - **Evolutionary**: **Incrementally refined** until it **becomes the final system**


> [!question] The requirement _"A user shall be able to search the appointments lists for all clinics"_ is not yet ready for design. List at least three clarifying questions you would ask the stakeholders ?
> > [!Answer]-
> > - Stakeholder may know what they **want**, but **not what is required**
> > - Stakeholders come from **different domain expertise**, so **understanding improves over time**.
> > - As stakeholders **understand the project better**, their **requirements naturally evolve.**
