

## What is a Birthday Attack

> [!information] Definition
> Find duplicate outputs (Collisions) in a hash function **much faster** than **normal guessing**

### The Birthday Paradox

> [!important] Definition
> In a room of just 23 people, there is a 50% chance that 2 people share the same birthday.
> a
> Instead of trying to match 1 specific target, an attackers finds **ANY 2 different inputs** that make the **exact same hash code**

> [!calculation] Efficiency
> If the **hash output** is **$n$** bits long, an attacker only needs to test about **$2^{n/2}$** to have a **50%** chance of finding a match.
