
# Overt vs Covert testing
| Feature   | Overt Testing             | Covert Testing              |
| --------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Stealth   | No attempt to hide tracks | Conducted in 'stealth' mode |
| Awareness | Target is typically aware | Aims to be undetected       |
| Objective | Thoroughness within scope | Simulates real-world attack |

# Definition

## Covert

> [!Definition]
> **Almost no one** inside the target organization (Including internal IT & Security defense teams) **know the test is happening**

### Key Characteristics

> [!tips] Hidden from Defenders
> **Unlike standard test** where the security teams are **fully briefed**, a **covert** test keeps defenders in the dark to see if they **spot and react to the mock attack**.

> [!tips] Realistic Simulations
> It **mimics** the **stealthy approach** a real-world malicious actor or an undetected insider trying to break systems

> [!tips] Measures Response Time
> **Tests how well** an organization's **monitoring tools**, **alert systems**, and **security personnel** detect and **handle unexpected threats.**

> [!tips] Higher Operational Risks
> Because internal teams treat the activity as a **genuine emergency**, it carries a **higher risk of disrupting** normal business resources during that response

## Overt

> [!Definition]
> Simulated cyberattack performed with **FULL knowledge**, **consent**, and **cooperation** of an organization's **IT and security staff**

### Key Characteristics

> [!tips] Full Transparency
> Internal IT team knows the **exact schedule**, **scope**, and **objectives** of the test

> [!tips] Insider Knowledge
> Testers often receive system diagrams, source code, credentials, or network maps ahead of time to evaluate systems comprehensively

> [!tips] Controlled Disruption
> Because defenders are **aware** of the activity, they can help **prevent operational downtime** and **distinguish** the test from a **real emergency**
> 

> [!tips] Collaborative Training
> Doubles as a **learning opportunity** for internal staff to watch how assessors **find and exploit weaknesses**
