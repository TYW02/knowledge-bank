
# High Loss on Training Data

> [!warning] Model
>- Model is too simple
>- Redesign your model

> [!debug] Optimization Problem
> - Gradient Descent (GD)
> - Stochastic GC (SGD)
> - Minibatch SGD 
> - Adam

## Shallower Networks

- Start from **shallower networks**, which are **easier to optimize**
- If **deeper networks** do not obtain **smaller training loss**, then there is an **optimization issue**

# When to stop training

> [!note] Convergence
> Train or fine-tune until **validation accuracy** or **loss stops** improving **over several epochs**
