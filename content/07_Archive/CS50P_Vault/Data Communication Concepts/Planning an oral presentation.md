

# Plan a presentation
- Before building slides: Plan !
- Presentation structure
	- Purpose 
	- Audience
	- Message


# Purpose
### What type of presentation ?

- Informative
	- Current number of negative and positive ratings and words associated with negative reviews

- Instructional
	- How to build the model for sentiment analysis

- Persuasive
	- Follow-up actions to revert the current situation of high number of negative ratings



# Audience
- Who is the audience ?
	- Technical colleagues
	- Managers or executive team
	- Customer

- How big is the audience ?
	- Small meeting
		- Meeting with 10 members of the financial team
	- Conference or large meeting
		- Meeting with 100 employees from the software development department
	- Workshop
		- Technical workshop for 30 customer's IT employees


# Message
### What is the central message ?

- After one week: 90% forgetten
- ==> What do we want to stick ?
- Opening statement
	- Capture audience's attention
		- Negative ratings scare customers away from our website

- Open statement
- Central message
	- One sentence
		- Delayed shipping is the main cause of negative reviews and immediate actions are needed to revert the situation.
- Closing statement
	- Sums up presentation and strengthens central message
		- There is a decrease in sales. Negative reviews have been increasing. Delayed shipping is causing negative ratings. Actions are needed to revert situation.


# Structure
- Introduction
	- Provide background attention
	- Catch audience attention
	- Glimpse of presentation content

- Methods, analysis and model output

- Conclusions and takeaways
	- Refes back to the introduction
	- Contains call-to-action statement or/and next steps


# Outline
- Graphs and visuals
- Sections (five or less smaller parts)
	1. Reason for analysis
	2. Exploratory analysis
	3. Sentiment analysis
	4. Conclusions
	5. Follow-up actions
