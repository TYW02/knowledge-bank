


# Why are stories needed ?
- The best results have no impact without proper presentation
- Convince change-adverse stakeholders
- Non-technical stakeholders



# What is data storytelling ?
> [!INFO] Data storytelling is the practice of building a narrative around a set of data and its accompanying visualizations to help convey the meaning of that data in a powerful and compelling fashion.

- Anecdotes = Imagination
- Stories = Memorable
- Add value (provide context)
- Capture audience's attention
- Facilitate decision-making
- Drive change
