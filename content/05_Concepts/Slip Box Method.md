# Fleeting Notes
Take notes randomly throughout the day
They dont have to have structure, just drop them anywhere


# Literature Notes
More structured, designed to be stored permanently

Take your fleeting notes and elaborate on them. Write them in your own words, to process and understand them so you remember long term.
Then keep in Reference Box

# Reference box
Collection of items you read


# Slip box
Concentrated on forming connection between notes


What does it add ?
What does it support ?
What does it contradict ?
Can you combine this with other notes ?
